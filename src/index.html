how I work

designI start with boundaries: which service owns the data, how services talk to each other and what happens when one of them fails. Only then do I write code.
problemsMost of my time goes into understanding the problem. When I know what the real problem is, I look for the simplest solution that works.
scaleAt scale, retries, slow dependencies and traffic spikes are normal, not edge cases. I plan for them early, and I measure before I optimise.
ownershipI own what I ship, from the first design to watching it in production and fixing it when it breaks.
reviewsI give clear, quick feedback and always explain why, so the team learns instead of just fixing the comment.
agentsI use AI coding agents every day. I choose the direction and review the result, and the agent does most of the typing.
