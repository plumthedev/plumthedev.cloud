how I work

designI start with boundaries. Which service owns the data, how services communicate and what happens when one of them fails. Then I write code.
problemsI spend most of the time understanding the problem. Once I know what it really is, I look for the simplest solution that solves it.
scaleThousands of bookings go through Telgani every day, so retries, slow dependencies and traffic spikes are normal. I plan for them early and measure before I optimise.
open mindI don't have a favourite pattern or tool. I use heavier approaches like event sourcing where consistency really needs them and keep things simple elsewhere. I prefer improving a system step by step over rewriting it.
agentsI use AI coding agents every day. I decide on the direction and review the result, and the agent does most of the typing.
